Privacy

Simple by design.

Your data stays local in this prototype.

XcelTap currently uses your browser's local storage. There is no XcelTap server, database, AI API, or account system in this prototype. Do not enter highly sensitive information such as passwords, bank details, Aadhaar or PAN numbers.

Future principle

You should know what information is being used, where it goes, and when it is shared. Automatic submission will not happen without user control.
